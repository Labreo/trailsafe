Effective Date: October 06, 2026 | Version 1.0

1. Nature of the System

TrailSafe is an experimental, open-source decision-support instrument and software dead-man switch engineered to assist solo hikers. It is not a certified life-safety device, satellite rescue beacon, or replacement for professional search-and-rescue equipment such as EPIRBs, PLBs, or satellite communicators.

2. Assumption of Inherent Outdoor Risks

Wilderness trekking, mountain scrambling, and remote backcountry navigation carry severe inherent hazards, including sudden hypothermia, dehydration, venomous wildlife encounters, flash flooding, falls from height, and death. You acknowledge that your decision to undertake solo backcountry journeys is entirely voluntary and that you assume full responsibility for your personal safety, physical conditioning, route planning, gear selection, and water supply.

3. Technical Limitations and Connectivity Blackouts

TrailSafe depends on consumer hardware, battery power, device operating systems, GPS satellite reception, and network infrastructure. The system may fail to transmit emergency alerts under conditions including, but not limited to:

  • Complete absence of cellular coverage or SMS gateway network partitions
  • Depletion of device battery or cold-temperature battery shutdowns
  • Physical impact damage, water submersion, or hardware destruction
  • Operating system background process termination or sleep throttling

4. Prior Labs TabPFN Probabilistic Model Output

Risk scores produced by Prior Labs TabPFN represent mathematical probabilities based on synthetic pre-training priors and historical tabular observations. They do not constitute certified medical diagnosis, physiological guarantees, or advice to proceed beyond physical limits. If you experience dizziness, disorientation, chest pain, or heat stroke symptoms, immediately seek shade, hydrate, and initiate emergency signaling.

5. False Alarms and Emergency Dispatch Costs

You agree to configure accurate emergency contact information and ensure designated contacts understand the operation of the dead-man switch. TrailSafe and its developers accept no liability for costs, search-and-rescue deployment expenses, civil penalties, or distress resulting from accidental escalations or false alarms triggered by missed check-ins.
