Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 06, 2026 | Version 1.0

1. Privacy Principles and Screen-Zero Philosophy

TrailSafe operates on an offline-first, privacy-preserving model. Outdoor navigation and biometric telemetry are processed locally on your hardware. We do not sell, monetize, or broker personal location data or health metrics to third parties or advertising networks.

2. Data Collection and Telemetry Scope

When you run TrailSafe during a solo hike, the system processes seven specific physical telemetry dimensions to compute exhaustion and dehydration hazard probabilities:

  • GPS Coordinates (Latitude, Longitude, Altitude, Moving Pace)
  • Cadence and Step Count via on-device accelerometer
  • Elapsed Active Time on trail
  • Ambient Environmental Temperature
  • Pack Fluid Volume remaining
  • Terrain Roughness and Elevation Delta

3. Prior Labs TabPFN In-Context Inference

Tabular risk evaluation is performed using Prior Labs TabPFN Bayesian foundation models. In local mode, model inference occurs entirely within the device Python runtime or client environment. No raw location or biometric streams are transmitted to external cloud servers unless you explicitly configure remote API endpoints.

4. Temporal Orchestration and State Durability

Dead-man switch countdowns and checkpoint states are maintained within your designated Temporal workflow cluster. Session records persist strictly for the duration of the hike to ensure reliable escalation if you become incapacitated or lose consciousness.

5. Emergency Alert Dispatch Protocols

If a checkpoint window expires and the safety grace period elapses without a verified check-in signal, or if you press the manual SOS panic trigger, TrailSafe transmits emergency dispatch payloads containing your last known GPS coordinates, trail leg, and risk metrics to your designated Telegram or SMS emergency contacts.

6. Local Storage and Data Retention

Trip logs and waypoint history are written to your local storage and an append-only JSONL ledger on your machine. You maintain complete control over these records and can delete them at any time.

7. Contact and Open-Source Inquiries

For inquiries regarding data protection or the open-source codebase, contact Kanak Sanjay Waradkar via the project repository at GitHub (@Labreo).
